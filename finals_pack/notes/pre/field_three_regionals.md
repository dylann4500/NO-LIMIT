# The field at three regionals, under one set of definitions (for `field.md`, `analyst` and `scout`)

Drafted Saturday 14 November 2026: Bay Area and Boston 15:25-15:55 PST, Pittsburgh added 15:55-16:15 PST as each
regional's raw hand files arrived. Notes only: the scripts were throwaway and have been deleted. Rates are counted as
in `stat_definitions.md` (team-equal means over teams that raise, the viewpoint team left out). **Grade A**
throughout for what was measured; the readings are **C**.

**What this is for.** `field.md` rests on one regional and one variant. The three regionals played three variants:
Bay Area three hole cards with a discard and an exposed card; Boston two hole cards and a flop chosen by a sealed
auction; Pittsburgh three hole cards, two boards and a discard after the turn. **A rate that came out the same under
all three is a property of the people and their agents, and is our safest prior for Sunday's fourth variant.** A rate
that differs is the variant, the field, or the days between the events.

In words:

1. **The field re-raises 9% in its first scored round and 14-17% in its last, and the top teams end at 24-28%.**
   The same climb under three variants.
2. **The big blind folds to an open 61-67% early and 58% in round 6 at all three; the top teams end at 40-43%**
   and re-raise 40-54% of opens.
3. **Short stacks shove first in 35-41% of the time at 10bb or less in the first round and 42-44% by round 3; the top
   teams about half the time.**
4. **From round 3 the whole field folds to a 3-bet 63-68%.**
5. **Limping is gone by round 3 (2%), opens are 32-37% of first-in chances, flop bets are raised 7-11% of the time.**
6. **Six to seven players are left at hand 40.**
7. **The direction of travel is the same everywhere:** more re-raises, more big-blind defence, more folds to 4-bets
   each round (section 2 has the levels), small blinds opening more.

## 3. What this changes in `field.md` (suggestions, grade C)

1. **Lean on section 1.** The crowded-styles shares in `field.md` section 1 are judgement; the eleven rows above are
   measured three times.
2. **Expect the counter-wave from the first scored round.** It arrived late and at the top at Boston and Pittsburgh,
   which send 14 of our 20 opponents; four of Pittsburgh's seven and three of Boston's seven were 4-betting 15%+ by
   their last round.
3. **Fold to a 4-bet is the rate that varies most between fields and is highest where teams re-raise wide.** Measure
   it pooled in practice 1 over the teams re-raising 25%+ (`field.md` section 3, row 3).
4. **The "wide pressure" class should not require wide opens.** By `field.md`'s thresholds (3-bet 25%+ with PFR 30%+)
   neither Boston's nor Pittsburgh's winner is in it: Du-o re-raised 39% and opened 23%; Pluto re-raised 25% and
   opened 25%. Count teams re-raising 25%+ and note their open rate separately.
5. **Postflop levels follow the variant.** C-bets, folds to bets, folds to a flop raise and later-street bets moved by
   10 points or more with the twist. Re-measure them in practice 1; do not carry a regional level.
6. **Round weights.** Boston and Pittsburgh used 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13; only Bay Area used 1 to 6 (Samuel's
   `research/regionals_web/REPORT.md`; the Finals' own weights are in `prep/PORTAL_SIGNED_IN_14NOV.md`).

## 4. Method and limits

- **Files.** Bay Area: `data/hands/`, about 20,150 tables a round, us at every table. Boston: `data/boston/`, about
  10,300 tables a round, another Boston team at every table. Pittsburgh: `data/pittsburgh/`, about 10,000 tables a
  round, a third team at every table. Which file is which round is worked out in `finalists.md` sections 5.1 and 6.1.
- **First round on file.** Bay Area and Boston: round 1. Pittsburgh has no round 1: its column is the 5,039-table
  practice between rounds 1 and 2 (round 2 itself: 3-bet 10, big blind folds 65, fold to 3-bet 65, fold to 4-bet 34).
- **Top group.** Bay Area: the official top 20 without us (19 teams). Boston: no official scores here, so the 20 teams
  with the highest weighted points at one team's tables (± 18 on totals of 520-730; the official top six are all in
  it). Pittsburgh: the official top 12.
- **Pittsburgh's viewpoint seat** was a calling station that busted before hand 40 at 48-80% of its tables. One seat
  in eight is that bot for the first 30-45 hands of every Pittsburgh table: early all-in collisions and shove calls
  there are raised by it, by an amount not measured.
- **Sample.** A team is seen at about 1,100 tables a round at Bay Area, 360 at Boston, 630 at Pittsburgh. The field
  means above carry under 1 point of sampling noise.
- **Depth bands and the hand-40 markers** assume 2,000 chips and blinds rising every 20 hands; all three regionals
  used that.
- **Early practices** are not like for like and are left out of the tables: Bay Area's practice 1 (fold to a 3-bet
  50%, 4-bets 23%), Boston's earliest file, probably its practice 2 (64% and 13%), none for Pittsburgh.
- **Not done:** Bay Area's rounds 2, 4 and 5 and Boston's rounds 2, 4 and 5 are in `finalists.md` Appendices B and H;
  no significance tests between regionals beyond the 5-point rule.

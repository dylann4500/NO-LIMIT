# The field at two regionals, under one set of definitions (for `field.md`, `analyst` and `scout`)

Drafted Saturday 14 November 2026, 15:25-15:55 PST, when Boston's raw hand files arrived. Notes only: the scripts
were throwaway and have been deleted. Rates are counted as in `stat_definitions.md` (team-equal means over teams
that raise, the viewpoint team left out). **Grade A** throughout for what was measured; the readings are **C**.

**What this is for.** `field.md` rests on one regional and one variant. Bay Area played three hole cards with a
discard and an exposed card; Boston played two hole cards with a flop chosen by a sealed auction. A rate that came
out the same under both is a property of the people and their agents, and should hold on Sunday. A rate that differs
is the variant, the field, or the three days between the events.

## 2. What matched at both regionals

Within 3 points in rounds 1, 3 and 6 (the pairs above):

- **How often the field re-raises**, and its rise through the night: 9%, 9-11%, 14-15%; top 20 at 24-26%.
- **The big blind against an open**: folds 66-67%, 62-65%, 58%; the top 20 at 40-43% by round 6, re-raising 40-43%.
- **Short-stack play**: first-in shoves at 10bb or less (35-36%, 40-42%, 42-44%; top 20 at 49-51%) and calls of
  those shoves (19-22%, 21%, 23-24%; top 20 at 27-28%).
- **Fold to a 3-bet from round 3 on**: 67-68% in the whole field at both.
- **C-bets and folds to bets on each street in the whole field**: c-bet 62%, 66-69%, 72-74%; flop folds 47-54%, turn
  38-47%, river 43-54% (largest gap 5, in round 1). Flop bets are raised 9-11% of the time everywhere.
- **Limping dies**: 3-5% in round 1, 2-3% after.
- **Table pace**: 0.9-1.3 early all-in collisions and 6.1-6.6 players left at hand 40 in every scored round.

So the direction `field.md` gives for rounds 1 to 6 (more re-raises, the big blind defending, more c-bets, folds to
bets rising a little, limps gone) held under two unrelated variants. (C: two events, same organisers and kit.)

## 4. What this changes in `field.md` (suggestions, grade C)

1. **The "wide pressure" class should not require wide opens.** By `field.md`'s thresholds (3-bet 25%+ with PFR 30%+)
   Boston's winner is not in it: Du-o re-raised 39% and opened 23%. Count teams re-raising 25%+ and note their
   open rate separately.
2. **Fold to a 4-bet is the rate that varies most between fields, and it is high wherever teams re-raise wide.**
   Measure it pooled in practice 1 over the teams re-raising 25%+ (`field.md` section 3, row 3): at Boston those teams
   folded far more: in the top 20 the median team folded 77% at Boston and 61% at Bay Area.
3. **Seat reading was more common at Boston.** With the cross-tabs of `finalists.md` Appendix C: re-raising a seat
   that has folded (+10 points or more) 3 teams of the top 20 at each regional; betting into folders (+20) 4 against
   5; stealing more from big blinds that fold (+12) 3 against 11. In the whole fields: 3, 9 and 9 of 126 teams at
   Bay Area against 5, 15 and 25 of 193 at Boston. (Bay Area counted on rounds 1, 3 and 6; Boston on all six.)
4. **Turn and river bet rates, and folds to a flop raise, moved with the variant.** Do not carry their levels to
   Sunday; re-measure them in practice 1.
5. **Everything in section 2 is the safest prior we have** for a third variant.

## 5. Method and limits

- **Files.** Bay Area: practice 1 and rounds 1, 3 and 6 of `data/hands/` (about 20,150 tables a round, us at every
  table). Boston: 13 files in `data/boston/`, every table with one Boston team (p-doom maximizers) at it; scored
  rounds are the first 10,000 iterations each (about 10,300 tables). Which file is which round is worked out in
  `finalists.md` section 5: rounds 1 and 2 are certain, the order of rounds 3 to 6 is very likely.
- **Sample.** A Boston team is seen at about 360 tables a round against 1,100 at Bay Area, so a single team's rare
  rates are twice as noisy; the field means in the table carry under 1 point of sampling noise either way.
- **Depth bands and the hand-40 markers** assume 2,000 chips and blinds rising every 20 hands; both regionals used
  that.
- **One viewpoint each.** Both sets show other teams only at one team's tables. Rates are safe; scores from these
  files sit below official ones (`first1000.md`), which is why Boston's top 20 is an estimate.
- **Not done:** Pittsburgh (no data yet); Bay Area's practice 2, which would be the like-for-like column for
  Boston's early practice.

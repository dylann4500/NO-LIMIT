# Our regional (we won it)
- Won with a steady loop: safe bot by minute 10, equity-and-ranges bot, then one tested change per round.
  Changing only kept us level: the top teams all lost about half a point a round as the field improved.
- What won tables: re-raising openers who had already folded to a re-raise at that table (we went from
  about 18% to 40-60% against them) and backing off after one call. Expect others to have copied this.
- The field's defence came by round 3: top teams folded only ~58% to re-raises and 4-bet ~20%. Don't build
  on "they fold to 3-bets".
- Practice rounds play whatever is active: our practice score was a fair read of the bot we had.

# Our regional
- The field got more aggressive every round (re-raises doubled). Tight bots that called off with strong
  hands did fine early and better late, but a bot that only folds busts around hand 100 and finishes 4th.
- Plan: tight, but punish wide re-raisers: 4-bet all-in and call re-raises wider against seats that
  re-raise a lot. Shove-or-fold when short. Don't be the seat that folds 85% to re-raises: everyone's bot
  now re-raises such seats at once.
